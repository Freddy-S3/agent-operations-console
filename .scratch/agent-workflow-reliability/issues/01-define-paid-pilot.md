# Define the paid pilot

Status: draft.

## Delivery

Define the fixed-scope Agent Workflow Reliability Setup and Team Enablement offer for one real repository workflow.

## Acceptance criteria

- The target customer is a 5-20 person software product team already using coding agents.
- The pilot names one operator, one repository workflow, and one primary operational problem.
- The deliverables include supervision, an approval gate, an evidence bundle, recovery from one failed run, and team enablement.
- The offer explains what Faruk performs manually and what the existing harness provides.
- The offer includes measurable success signals such as recovery time, abandoned-run rate, verified completion, or review rework.
- The offer states explicit exclusions so it does not imply a broad SaaS platform or generic AI consultancy.
- A provisional price hypothesis is recorded for interview testing rather than presented as a final price.
