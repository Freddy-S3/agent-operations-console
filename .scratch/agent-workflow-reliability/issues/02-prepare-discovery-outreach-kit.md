# Prepare the discovery and outreach kit

Status: draft.

## Delivery

Prepare the materials required to research and contact the first 15 plausible buyer teams without relying on improvisation.

## Acceptance criteria

- The kit names the buyer role and the reason that person can approve a pilot.
- A research list contains 15 plausible software teams across English-speaking Canada and the United States that fit the target profile.
- The interview guide asks about recent agent-assisted work, failure or rework, current workaround, cost, approval process, and recovery behavior.
- The outreach message requests a learning conversation rather than pretending to sell a finished product.
- Follow-up messages, a conversation tracker, and a simple evidence summary template exist.
- The kit distinguishes observed customer facts from Faruk's assumptions.
