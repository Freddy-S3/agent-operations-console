# Publish the minimal credibility website

Status: draft.

## Delivery

Publish a one-page public site that helps a qualified software team understand the pilot and request a conversation.

## Acceptance criteria

- The page identifies the target team without requiring visitors to understand the word “harness.”
- The page states the operational outcome in terms of recoverable, reviewable, and safe-to-approve agent-assisted delivery.
- The page describes the fixed-scope pilot and team enablement clearly.
- The page includes only public harness material, synthetic data, or explicitly approved proof.
- The page includes one working discovery-call or contact action.
- The page makes no unvalidated customer, revenue, or performance claims.
- The page includes a concise privacy notice before any optional analytics or research telemetry is enabled.

Task 1: Define the paid pilot.
