# Run discovery and secure the first paid pilot

Status: draft.

## Delivery

Conduct targeted discovery conversations and offer the concierge pilot when the buyer's problem matches the defined workflow.

## Acceptance criteria

- Outreach is attempted with 15 plausible teams across English-speaking Canada and the United States that fit the target profile.
- Each conversation records recent behavior, current workaround, cost or risk, purchasing authority, and reaction to the pilot outcome.
- The evidence distinguishes repeated problems from isolated feature requests.
- At least one conversation produces a qualified opportunity for a paid pilot, or the record explains why the offer needs to change.
- No mass outreach, public launch, or broad product build is used as a substitute for learning.
- The discovery result is summarized as continue, revise, or stop with evidence for the decision.

## Blocked by

Tasks 1 and 2: Define the paid pilot and Prepare the discovery and outreach kit.
